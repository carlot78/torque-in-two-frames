Torque in Two Frames

A box carries its own body frame {b}. A force f acts at a point r on the box. Move the body, rotate it, move the push point, and watch the rotation matrix, the skew matrices and the torque m = r × f = [r]f update in both frames. Notation follows Modern Robotics (Lynch & Park): [x] is the 3×3 skew-symmetric matrix of x.
